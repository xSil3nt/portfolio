How I work

I'm a Computer Engineering graduate. My work spans sensor firmware, satellite telemetry, robotics, and simulation.

02SWOT analysis

Current strengths
and gaps.

S / Strengths

Connecting hardware and software

I work on both sensor firmware and the software receiving its readings.

W / Weaknesses

More breadth than depth

Most of my work is at the prototype stage. I need more experience with production, compliance, and costing.

O / Opportunities

Taking existing projects further

I can develop the robotics projects further and learn through code reviews.

T / Threats

Limited hardware, data, and time

Limited hardware and field data make it harder to check simulation results.

What I'll change

Write requirements and tests before coding. Include costs and relevant standards, then get the results reviewed.
